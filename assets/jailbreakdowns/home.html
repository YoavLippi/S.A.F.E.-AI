Welcome

Large Language Models (LLMs) are currently a huge industry, and they've been implemented in a huge amount of areas. Not only that, but we as consumers use them at an incredible rate. For example, ChatGPT was the most downloaded app in 2025, with around 770 million downloads. (Business of apps)

However, I am of the belief that the integration of these models is entirely too quick, and the safeguards implemented in them are not robust or consistent enough. This research project, and by extension this website, aims to prove that the GPT-oss-20b and GPT-oss-120b models are not safe to use in high-security operations, because their protocols are not foolproof.

Above this entry, there are a series of buttons. Each one will show you a method of jailbreaking the model. Remember that the LLM may not respond the same way every time, so try your solutions more than once, and use the reasoning trace to see where you can improve.
